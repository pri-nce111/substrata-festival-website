// 03 — INFO

This isn't
a festival.
It's a frequency.

SUBSTRATA started in an unlicensed basement in 2019 with two speakers and forty people who found out by word of mouth. It still runs on that logic. No sponsors on the walls. No main-stage pyrotechnics. Just a sound system built to move air, and a lineup that answers to the floor, not the algorithm.

Three nights. One location, disclosed 48 hours before doors. Bring earplugs, bring water, bring whoever you trust to get you home.
